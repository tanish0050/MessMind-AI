# MessMind AI

## Smart Food Waste Prediction & Reduction System

MessMind AI is an AI-powered sustainability project designed to help
college messes predict food waste and make better food preparation
decisions.

## SDG Alignment

Primary SDG:

SDG 12 - Responsible Consumption and Production

Secondary SDGs:

SDG 2 - Zero Hunger
SDG 13 - Climate Action

## Problem

College messes often prepare food based on estimated attendance.
Variations in attendance and menu preferences can result in excess
food and avoidable food waste.

## Proposed Solution

MessMind AI uses Machine Learning to predict expected leftover food
based on:

- Day
- Meal
- Menu
- Expected number of students

The system then provides a waste-risk level and a recommendation
to support better food preparation.

## Machine Learning

Algorithm:

Random Forest Regression

Target:

Leftover food in kilograms

## Responsible AI

The system:

- Avoids collecting personal student information
- Explains that predictions are based on historical data
- Keeps humans responsible for final decisions
- Requires representative and reliable data
- Should not be treated as a replacement for human judgment
